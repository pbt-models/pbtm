# pbtm: Population-Based Threshold Models of Seed Germination

Fits population-based threshold models (PBTMs) to seed germination time
courses, as described by Bradford and Bello (2022)
[doi:10.19103/AS.2022.0105.05](https://doi.org/10.19103/AS.2022.0105.05)
. Supports thermal time, hydrotime, hydrothermal time, hydropriming,
hydrothermal priming, aging, promoter and inhibitor models, optional
mixtures of seed subpopulations, germination speed calculations, and
plots of the fitted models on linear or linearized (log time, probit
fraction) scales.
